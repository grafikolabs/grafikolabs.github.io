A still frame can only ever show you one scene.

Scenes, tunable white, cove and cabinet layers, sun-tracking shades, keypad presets. None of it reads in a photograph, and none of it can be demonstrated in a five-minute walkthrough on possession day — which is exactly when the owner is least able to absorb it.

The room in front of you has not reloaded, moved or cut. Only the light changed. That is the entire product, and it is the one thing a brochure cannot carry.

Seven addressable layers, one press.

Every fixture in this room belongs to a layer, and every layer carries its own intensity and colour temperature — the same way the scene sits in the processor on site. Watch the readout while you change scenes: nothing is faded globally, each layer travels on its own curve to its own target.

      10

      Scope definitions

      Camera-frustum-limited modelling

      We model, texture and light only what the camera sees. Geometry outside the frustum is not built; geometry that appears briefly or at distance is built to an appropriate level of detail.

      This is a deliberate efficiency decision and it is what makes this price point possible — the entire budget is concentrated on what appears on screen at full quality rather than spread across a complete unseen building. The trade-off: the assets are purpose-built for the approved camera path, not a general-purpose model of the apartment.

      This is why camera path changes after Gate 1 are chargeable — a new path reveals geometry that was never built.

      If Lutron requires fully-built reusable spaces for VR, configurators or future re-shooting, we quote that separately as a Full Build option at approximately +45% on the space rate.
